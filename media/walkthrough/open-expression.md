Place the cursor inside any expression tag and open the editor:

```xml
<textFieldExpression><![CDATA[$F{status}.equals("PAID")]]></textFieldExpression>
```

You can use the **JRXML: Open Expression Editor** command, the pencil icon in the
editor title bar, or `Ctrl+Alt+E`. The panel keeps your unsaved edits if you switch
away and come back to the same expression.
